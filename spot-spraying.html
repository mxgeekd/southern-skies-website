Spot spraying

Target weeds without treating the whole paddock

Efficient, controlled application focused only where it's needed.

Precision where it matters

Spot spraying allows targeted treatment of problem areas without blanket spraying entire paddocks. This reduces chemical use, protects pasture, and improves overall efficiency.

Ideal for early infestations, patch control, and ongoing weed management strategies.

Where it works well

  • Weed patches in otherwise healthy pasture
  • Fence lines and isolated infestations
  • Maintaining pasture quality
  • Reducing chemical costs

Value

  • Lower chemical usage
  • Reduced impact on desirable species
  • Improved long-term pasture management
  • More efficient than full paddock treatment
