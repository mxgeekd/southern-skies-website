Our story

Useful technology, without the sales pitch.

Southern Skies Agritech is a locally owned agricultural drone service supporting farms across the Mid North Coast of NSW.

I grew up in this region before spending the last couple of decades working in the military and in technical roles, including large-scale infrastructure projects. That experience has shaped how I approach this business — practical, structured, and focused on getting the job done properly.

Southern Skies Agritech wasn’t started to push new technology for the sake of it. It exists to provide a useful option when timing, access, or conditions make traditional methods difficult. Whether that’s wet paddocks, steep ground, or tight spray windows, the goal is simple — help get the work done efficiently without adding complexity.

This is a service built around reliability and straightforward communication. No overpromising, no unnecessary jargon — just a practical approach to supporting farm operations where drone application makes sense.
